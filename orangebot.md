OrangeBot is an AI assistant application, operated by Jeremy Wildfire (doing business as OrangeBot), that you use by text message.

OrangeBot is software that runs on a dedicated server. Authorized users text it questions and commands, and the application automatically replies with answers, reminder and calendar confirmations, and notifications when background software tasks finish. Every reply is generated by the OrangeBot software; no person types the messages.

## Get started by text

Text START to (984) 401-8690 to enroll in OrangeBot text messages.

By texting START, you agree to receive automated text messages from OrangeBot (Jeremy Wildfire) in reply to your requests. Message frequency varies with your requests. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP at any time to cancel. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Access is limited to authorized users. Messages from numbers that are not authorized are not processed.

## What the messages look like

- You text: START
- OrangeBot: "OrangeBot Personal Assistant: You're opted in to assistant texts. Msg frequency varies with your requests. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."
- You text: "Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow at 9"
- OrangeBot: "OrangeBot: Done - reminder set for tomorrow at 9:00am: call the dentist. Reply STOP to opt out."
