What changed

+20%

more pull requests in our public repos than by this date last year, bots left out. Issues opened are up 21%. Releases are down 22%. Not a controlled productivity study.

<none yet> DRAFT, 2026-10-07. Every word on this slide is draft wording for Jeremy to replace; the numbers are sourced in slide25-activity-sources.md and slide25-activity-data.json, beside this file. Counted on 2026-10-07 between 11:42 and 11:52 UTC from GitHub's public API.

What is counted: the 25 public repositories of the gsm and OpenRBQM ecosystem (GitHub organisations Gilead-Public, Gilead-BioStats and OpenRBQM). Same calendar window in both years: 1 January to 6 October, in UTC. Issues and pull requests opened by bot accounts are left out (dependency updates, workflow-template updates, scheduled reports: 4 pull requests in the 2025 window, 58 in 2026, and 20 issues in 2026).
• Releases published (tags that look like a version): 60 → 47 (−22%). Whoever published them, people or a workflow.
• Issues opened: 491 → 594 (+21%). With bot accounts counted too: 491 → 614 (+25%).
• Issues closed: 425 → 466 (+10%). With bot accounts counted too: 425 → 486 (+14%).
• Pull requests opened: 403 → 484 (+20%). With bot accounts counted too: 407 → 542 (+33%).
• Pull requests merged: 359 → 401 (+12%). With bot accounts counted too: 363 → 437 (+20%).

The June post against the public record. The post (diary #1, 2026-06-10) said releases were up roughly 50% and issues and pull requests had roughly doubled. On public data, 1 January to 9 June, 2026 against 2025: releases 31 → 27 (−13%), issues opened 314 → 381 (+21%), issues closed 229 → 306 (+34%), pull requests opened 246 → 282 (+15%), merged 218 → 233 (+7%). Nothing doubled. The release figure does come back on a different window: the 12 months to 9 June 2026 against the 12 months before, 41 → 62 (+51%). That window is helped by March 2025, when one package became several and each started releasing on its own. The post may also have counted repositories that are not public; that cannot be checked from here.

What moves the numbers (say it or not, Jeremy's call)
• One repository, qcthat, went from 4 issues opened to 133. Without it, issues opened are 487 → 461 (−5%) and pull requests opened 401 → 431 (+8%).
• One repository, gsm.app, went quiet in public: 147 issues opened in the 2025 window, 6 in 2026. Without it, issues opened are 344 → 588 (+71%) and pull requests opened 322 → 481 (+49%).
• Releases fell partly because 2025 held the first releases of the new packages, and gsm.app and gsm.qc published 13 releases in the 2025 window and none in 2026.
• People: 14 accounts opened an issue or pull request in the 2025 window, 12 in 2026. That is a count of accounts, not of team size.

The chart: pull requests opened in each whole month, January to September. 2025: 39, 36, 75, 39, 42, 66, 21, 15, 58. 2026: 46, 51, 47, 52, 69, 46, 61, 61, 49. The headline number also counts 1 to 6 October (12 in 2025, 2 in 2026).
