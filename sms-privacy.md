**Personal Assistant text messaging service, operated by OrangeBot**

Last updated: September 23, 2026

OrangeBot operates a private text messaging assistant at (984) 401-8690. The service exists for the personal use of its owner and is not offered to the public.

## What data we collect

- The phone number that texts the service, used only to confirm the sender is the authorized owner.
- The content of messages sent to and from the service, used only to answer questions, manage the owner's reminders and calendar, and pass tasks to the owner's own software tools.
- Basic delivery records (timestamps and delivery status) from our SMS provider, Twilio, used to keep the service working.

## How data is used

Message data is used only to provide the assistant service to its owner. Messages from any number other than the owner's are ignored. The service does not send marketing or promotional messages.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Message content is processed by the service providers needed to run the assistant (Twilio for SMS delivery and Anthropic for AI processing) and is not shared with anyone else.

## Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help.
